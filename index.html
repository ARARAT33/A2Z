THE A2Z PRINCIPLE

Innovation is a shared resource.

AWE does not claim ownership of ideas published in A2Z. A2Z is a public idea and innovation archive. A contribution may describe anything from an invention or scientific concept to an educational method, product concept, artistic innovation or other new possibility. Contributors must present their own contribution through a dedicated web page and agree that the submitted idea is intended for free public use. AWE does not guarantee originality or legal status.
